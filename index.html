Lesotho holds the unique distinction of being the only independent nation in the world situated entirely above one thousand meters in elevation. Known globally as the Kingdom in the Sky, this mountainous enclave is completely encircled by South Africa. Its dramatic topography is shaped by the majestic Maloti and Drakensberg ranges, creating landscapes that range from sweeping alpine plateaus to deep, rugged river gorges.
The climate is defined by distinct seasons, featuring warm summers and bitterly cold, dry winters where snowfall is common in the highlands. Beyond its natural beauty, the nation is water-rich, with pristine rivers feeding large-scale hydro-electric projects. Culturally, the Basotho people maintain vibrant traditions, famously symbolized by the colorful and durable woven blankets worn for warmth and ceremony. Education is a priority, resulting in one of the highest literacy rates on the continent.
The geological diversity is also remarkable, with prehistoric dinosaur footprints preserved in the rock, offering an incredible link to the land's deep natural history.
