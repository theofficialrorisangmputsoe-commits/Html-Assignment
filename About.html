About Lesotho

The Kingdom in the Sky

Lesotho is a completely landlocked enclave entirely surrounded by South Africa. It stands as the only independent state globally situated entirely above one thousand meters in elevation.

Rugged Terrain

The terrain is extraordinarily mountainous, dominated by the Maloti and Drakensberg ranges. These soaring peaks create dramatic vistas, with snow frequently blanketing the highlands during winter.

Water Resources

Water is Lesotho's most vital natural resource. Pristine mountain rivers are captured in massive dams, generating hydroelectric power and supplying water to the region.

Culture & Heritage

The Basotho people share a resilient heritage. The traditional woven blanket serves not only for warmth during cold nights but as an enduring symbol of national identity.

Climate & Education

The climate features pleasant summers and cold, dry winters with significant highlands snowfall. Additionally, Lesotho maintains high literacy rates and supports both Sesotho and English as official languages.

Ancient History

The region preserves a remarkable natural history, featuring numerous prehistoric dinosaur footprints embedded in the rocks that offer a direct link to the ancient past.
